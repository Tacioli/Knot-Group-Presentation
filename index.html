Instructions:
1. Sketch the knot diagram with left mouse clicks.
2. Move the mouse to the first drawn point to close the diagram.
3. The system will clear a small region at each intersection. Choose which arc passes over.
4. Click the "Group Presentation" button to calculate the knot invariants.

How to read the Group Presentation:
In Knot Theory, each continuous arc of the diagram (the segment of the curve going from one undercrossing to the next undercrossing) corresponds to a generator of the fundamental group, denoted by xi.
The numbering of the generators (x1, x2, ..., xn) follows the exact order in which the segments were drawn on the plane. The algorithm extracts the relations crossing by crossing using the Wirtinger Presentation.
